FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before starting a conversation with NTS. Can't find your answer? Just reach out directly.

How long does a typical project take?
+

Project timelines vary significantly based on scope and complexity. A focused module or integration typically takes 4–8 weeks. A full enterprise platform build ranges from 3–9 months. We define clear milestones and delivery schedules at the start of every engagement, so you always know exactly where things stand.

Do you work with startups or only enterprise clients?
+

Our expertise is enterprise-grade systems, but we work with organizations at various stages — from well-funded startups building their core platform to large corporations transforming legacy infrastructure. The key is that you need something serious built, and we're the team to build it.

What is your engagement model?
+

We operate on fixed-scope project engagements with clear deliverables, timelines, and pricing agreed upfront. For ongoing development and support, we also offer dedicated team retainer arrangements. Every engagement begins with a discovery phase to ensure full alignment before any development begins.

Do you provide post-launch support?
+

Yes. All projects include a post-launch stabilization period. Beyond that, we offer structured maintenance and support plans covering monitoring, performance optimization, security updates, and feature enhancements. We build long-term client relationships, not one-off deliveries.

Are your platforms available in Arabic?
+

Yes. Bilingual (English/Arabic) support with full RTL layout is a standard capability at NTS. We design for both languages from day one — not as an afterthought — ensuring interfaces feel native in both directions for users across the MENA region.
